The “Demand Support” Excuse

Common excuse to not share code:
“People will pick holes and demand support.”


  • Suppose you’ve written an R/Python package that you want to share via GitHub.
  • You are worried that users will demand technical support.

Silently think (and write) about two boundaries that you would set to limit how much support you are willing to give.

Common excuse to not share code:

Discussion (part 3)

The tech firm (Team A) refuses to open-source its proprietary stress-testing code due to commercial secrets.

But the public safety board (Team B) refuses to trust a black box.


How can Team A prove its code works without leaking its competitive edge to global rivals?

Discussion (part 4)
